Hi, we are Nirakar and Pratimesh

  • Both of us shoot every booking. No stranger turns up on your day.
  • Photos and reel from one team, so the look stays the same.
  • Prices are told upfront on WhatsApp. No hidden charges.
  • We stay till the last ritual, not till the clock runs out.
  • We talk to your family beforehand, so nobody is surprised.

It started in 2019 at my cousin's wedding. The hired photographer cancelled two days before, so we borrowed a camera from an uncle and shot it ourselves. Our relatives still ask for those photos.

After that, one family told another, and another. We learned lighting from night functions and haldi mornings, and we learned that the best frame is usually a grandmother wiping her eyes, not the posed group photo.

Today NP Photography is still the two of us, a few cameras and a lot of chai. Message us your date and we will tell you honestly if we can do it justice.
